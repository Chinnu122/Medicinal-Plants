## Getting Started

The main navigation menu provides access to:
- **Home**: Return to the homepage

## Browsing Plants

### Plant Catalog Page

The Plants page offers powerful search and filtering capabilities:

#### Search Functionality
1. **Text Search**
   - Use the search bar at the top of the page
   - Search by:
     - Plant name (e.g., "Turmeric", "Ginger")
     - Scientific name (e.g., "Curcuma longa")
     - Benefits (e.g., "anti-inflammatory", "digestive")
     - Uses (e.g., "arthritis", "pain relief")

2. **Search Tips**
   - Enter multiple keywords for better results
   - Search is case-insensitive
   - Results update in real-time as you type

#### Filtering Options

Click the "Filters" button to access advanced filtering:

1. **Category Filter**
   - Anti-inflammatory
   - Digestive
   - Skin
   - Respiratory
   - Immune Support
   - Pain Relief
   - And more...

2. **Availability Filter**
   - **Common**: Easily available year-round
   - **Moderate**: Seasonally available
   - **Rare**: Limited availability

3. **Difficulty Filter**
   - **Easy**: Simple to prepare and use
   - **Moderate**: Requires some knowledge
   - **Difficult**: Advanced preparation needed

4. **Sort Options**
   - Name (A-Z)
   - Scientific Name
   - Availability (Common to Rare)
   - Difficulty (Easy to Difficult)

#### View Modes

Switch between two display modes:
- **Grid View**: Visual cards showing plant images
- **List View**: Compact list with essential information

#### Plant Cards

Each plant card displays:
- High-quality plant image
- Common and scientific names
- Availability status badge
- Price for dried form
- Brief description
- Top 3 benefits
- "Learn More" button to view full details

### Plant Detail Page

Click on any plant to view comprehensive information:

#### Overview Section
- Full description of the plant
- Large, detailed image
- Scientific classification
- Price information (fresh, dried, supplements)
- Availability and difficulty ratings
- Growing season and regions

#### Benefits & Uses
- Complete list of health benefits
- Specific medical and wellness uses
- Scientifically verified information

#### Preparation Methods
- Step-by-step preparation instructions
- Different forms (tea, powder, paste, etc.)
- Dosage recommendations
- Storage tips

#### Precautions & Safety
- Important safety warnings
- Potential side effects
- Drug interactions
- Contraindications
- Who should avoid this plant

#### Shopping Actions
- Add to cart in desired quantity
- Choose form (fresh, dried, supplement)
- Save to favorites

## Using the AI Assistant

### Accessing the AI Assistant

1. Click "AI Assistant" in the navigation menu
2. Or click the chat icon from any page

### How to Interact

#### Asking Questions

The AI Assistant can help with:

1. **Plant Recommendations**
   - "What plant is good for headaches?"
   - "I need something for better sleep"
   - "Recommend plants for digestive issues"

2. **Specific Plant Information**
   - "Tell me about turmeric benefits"
   - "How do I prepare ginger tea?"
   - "What are the side effects of ashwagandha?"

3. **Usage Guidance**
   - "How much turmeric should I take daily?"
   - "Can I combine ginger and turmeric?"
   - "What's the best time to take herbs?"

4. **Growing & Sourcing**
   - "How do I grow basil at home?"
   - "Where can I buy organic herbs?"
   - "What's the difference between fresh and dried?"

#### Voice Features

1. **Voice Input**
   - Click the microphone icon
   - Speak your question clearly
   - The AI will transcribe and respond

2. **Voice Output**
   - Toggle "Read Aloud" to hear responses
   - Adjust voice speed in settings
   - Available in multiple languages

#### Tips for Best Results

- **Be Specific**: "Plants for chronic knee pain" works better than "pain relief"
- **Provide Context**: Mention any allergies or medical conditions
- **Follow Up**: Ask clarifying questions if needed
- **Use Natural Language**: Talk naturally, as you would with a person

### AI Assistant Limitations

⚠️ **Important Disclaimers**:
- AI provides general information only
- Not a substitute for medical advice
- Always consult healthcare professionals
- Verify information before use

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Maximum Benefit

1. **Start with Common Plants**
   - Begin with easily available herbs
   - Learn proper preparation methods
   - Observe how your body responds

2. **Keep a Journal**
   - Note which plants you try
   - Record effects and benefits
   - Track any reactions

3. **Consult Professionals**
   - Discuss with your doctor
   - Especially if taking medications
   - Get proper diagnosis first

4. **Quality Matters**
   - Buy from reputable sources
   - Look for organic options
   - Check expiration dates

5. **Proper Storage**
   - Keep dried herbs in airtight containers
   - Store in cool, dark places
   - Label with purchase dates

### Safety First

✅ **Do's**:
- Research thoroughly before use
- Start with small doses
- Monitor for reactions
- Read all precautions
- Store safely away from children

❌ **Don'ts**:
- Don't self-diagnose serious conditions
- Avoid during pregnancy without medical advice
- Don't exceed recommended doses
- Never mix with medications without approval
- Don't use expired products

### Making the Most of HerbWise

1. **Explore Regularly**
   - New plants added frequently
   - Updated information
   - Seasonal recommendations

2. **Use Multiple Features**
   - Combine AI chat with database browsing
   - Save favorites for quick access
   - Read community reviews

3. **Stay Informed**
   - Check the blog for articles
   - Subscribe to newsletters
   - Follow social media for tips

4. **Provide Feedback**
   - Rate plants you've tried
   - Share your experiences
   - Report any issues

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't find a specific plant?**
- Try different keywords

**AI Assistant not responding?**
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache

**Cart not updating?**
- Refresh the page
- Log in to your account
- Check browser cookies

**Search not working?**
- Clear filters
- Reset search
- Check spelling
