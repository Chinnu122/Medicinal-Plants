# 🌿 HerbWise Project Information

## Project Overview

**HerbWise** is a comprehensive web application designed to democratize access to medicinal plant knowledge and natural healing solutions. The platform combines traditional herbal wisdom with modern AI technology to help users discover, learn about, and source affordable medicinal plants.

## Mission Statement

To make natural healing accessible and affordable for everyone by providing:
- Comprehensive, scientifically-verified information about medicinal plants
- Intelligent AI-powered guidance for plant selection and usage
- Affordable sourcing options for quality herbal remedies
- A user-friendly platform that bridges ancient wisdom with modern technology

## Problem Statement

### Challenges in Natural Medicine Access

1. **Information Overload**: Scattered and often unreliable information about medicinal plants across the internet
2. **Accessibility**: Limited access to expert herbalist knowledge, especially in remote areas
3. **Cost Barriers**: Premium natural remedies and supplements are often expensive
4. **Safety Concerns**: Lack of clear guidance on proper usage, dosages, and contraindications
5. **Quality Assurance**: Difficulty in finding authentic, high-quality herbal products

### Our Solution

HerbWise addresses these challenges by:
- Centralizing verified information about 100+ medicinal plants
- Providing free AI-powered consultation and recommendations
- Highlighting affordable sourcing options and cost-effective alternatives
- Ensuring all information is expert-verified with clear safety guidelines
- Connecting users with trusted suppliers and quality products
